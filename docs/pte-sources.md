# Sources for the daily plan

This note records the public pages used for labels in the app. It is not visa advice, not a PTE mock, and it does not include exam questions or sample answers.

## Superior English score record

Page: [Superior English](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language/superior-english)

Table 1 applies to tests taken on or after 7 August 2025. For PTE Academic, each skill has its own minimum: Listening 69, Reading 70, Writing 85, Speaking 88. The same table lists IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training as 8 in each component. That is the “四项 8” equivalence. It is not a PTE score of 80, and it is not Pearson’s concordance table.

Table 2, for PTE Academic taken on or before 6 August 2025, lists 79 in each component. The 22 November 2026 booking is after 7 August 2025, so the app’s initial numbers are the Table 1 figures. The user can edit them. The app does not rewrite an edited 79 back to the new table.

The page also says some visas accept other evidence, and it does not accept fully online tests. The app does not decide visa eligibility.

## PTE Academic task names

- [Updates to PTE Academic](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-updates-2025/): from tests taken after 7 August 2025, PTE Academic and PTE Academic UKVI add Summarize Group Discussion and Respond to a Situation in Speaking & Writing. PTE Core is unchanged. The original 20 question types remain. The test is still about two hours. This app does not map those 20 types into a study plan.
- [Test Tips PDF, July 2025](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/Test-Tips-New-Speaking-Questions-2025.pdf): Summarize Group Discussion — after the discussion, 10 seconds to prepare and 2 minutes to respond. Respond to a Situation — 40 seconds to respond after the prompt audio.
- [The Enhanced PTE Academic research report](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/research-report-pdfs/2025-07--enhanced-pte-a-report.pdf): Respond to a Situation also states 10 seconds to prepare and 40 seconds to respond. Summarize Group Discussion states a discussion of about 2 to 3 minutes and up to 2 minutes to retell.

The app names these tasks only in a citation. Shadowing and the microphone score are not these tasks and are not a PTE score.

## Exam day

- Booking read from the user’s Pearson email: Sunday 22 November 2026, 15:30 AEDT, Box Hill VIC.
- 15:00 is the latest arrival, not the start. [PTE Test Center Rules](https://www.pearsonpte.com/policy-center/test-center-rules/): “You must arrive at least 30 minutes before your test and sign in at the test center reception. If you arrive late you will not be allowed to take the test and you will lose your test fee.”
- The Saturday 21 November 23:00 to Sunday 22 November 07:00 night shift stays as a normal shift in Melbourne. In November, Melbourne and Sydney use the same AEDT clock. The app does not plan wake-up, departure, or a route, and it does not treat the usual Sunday sleep until 14:30 as safe on this date.
- 21 November: no practice; prepare ID and how you will get there.
- 22 November: no new words and no study. Sleep and transport come first.
- No notifications were added.

## What the app does not do

No authentic PTE items, no official scoring, no readiness percentage, and no use of browser speech recognition as a skill diagnosis or PTE score. Baseline scores were not available.
